Subject: Issue 01: The question no one asks about money

Hello,

Most families ask whether they can afford something. Very few ask a better question first: what is this money actually for.

Not in the abstract. Specifically. If every dollar that passed through your household this month had a label on it, first, second, or third, generation, would you know what most of them said.

The Financial and Resources pillar does not begin with a budget. It begins with a decision about what money is for in your family line: survival, comfort, or something being built that will still be standing after you are gone. Budgets are just the paperwork that follows that decision. Without it, they rarely last past February.

Try this. Before your next paycheck arrives, write one sentence answering the question above. Not a plan. Just a sentence about what the money is for. Keep it somewhere you will see it when you spend.

Carrying the calling with you this week,
The CLANOR Team
